ARCHITECTURAL EVALUATION & BOUNDARY DECISION:
1. When is ArrayList a reasonable engineering choice?
   - When operations are predominantly random access (get(k)), append at end,
   - and full sequential iteration, benefiting from contiguous L1/L2 cache prefetching.
2. When to STOP recommending ArrayList?
   - When frequent insertions/removals occur at the FRONT or MIDDLE of large lists,
   - because System.arraycopy forces O(n) element shifting per operation.
3. Changed Condition Response (Up Next Queue):
   - ArrayDeque is strictly superior to both LinkedList and ArrayList.
   - It provides O(1) amortized add/remove at BOTH ends without pointer node overhead.
