--- 4. Starvation Simulation & Fair Aging Rule ---
Problem: A Severity-5 patient (e.g. minor cough) may suffer from infinite starvation
if critical patients (Severity 1-2) keep arriving continuously.
Fair Rule: For every 30 minutes of waiting time, the patient's effective severity
is promoted by 1 level (e.g. effectiveSeverity = max(1, severity - waitMinutes/30)).
This guarantees an upper bound on waiting time and eliminates starvation!
